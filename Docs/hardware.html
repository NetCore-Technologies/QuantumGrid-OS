THE HARDWARE LAYERS

What QuantumGrid
is built around.

⌘

Qualcomm platform

IPQ8074 hardware, device tree and upstream qualcommax/ipq807x target. NSS/PPE claims remain gated on compatible drivers and measured validation.

◌

5G connectivity

The RG500Q-EA modem is part of the AW1000 platform. Management must respect modem firmware, AT/QMI interfaces and carrier configuration.

⌕

RJ11 phone port

A single analogue FXS port connected to a Skyworks Si32261 SLIC. The community driver bridges modem PCM audio and the phone interface.

SPECIAL FEATURE / RJ11

Your old phone
has a place here.

QuantumGrid integrates the AW1000 FXS driver, fxsd daemon and native Phone (RJ11) LuCI panel. A VoLTE SIM and separately obtained Skyworks DSP patch are required.
