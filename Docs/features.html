PLATFORM / CAPABILITIES

Designed for the
whole network.

QuantumGrid brings major router systems into one operating model. It is built around OpenWrt services and AW1000 hardware — not disconnected demo toggles.

01
FOUNDATION

Network & WAN

Interface status, IPv4/IPv6 default routes and policy-routing rules, multi-WAN visibility, DNS, DHCP and diagnostics. Route and rule inventories are read-only kernel snapshots; they do not simulate packet paths or verify Multi-WAN health.

WAN/LAN visibility · mwan3 · ping · traceroute · mtr
02
FOUNDATION

Wi-Fi intelligence

A hardware-aware wireless layer for Qualcomm radios, with channel, client, band and roaming visibility.

Wi-Fi 6 · radio diagnostics · DAWN integration path
03
ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Gaming & latency

Configured SQM/CAKE queues and read-only qdisc counters, plus an on-demand five-packet fixed-target ICMP diagnostic reporting packet loss and RTT variation. Controlled-load bufferbloat tests and active prioritisation policies remain in development.

SQM · CAKE · qdisc telemetry · measured ping loss/jitter
04
HARDWARE INTEGRATION

5G & voice

Cellular modem management for RG500Q-EA, plus the AW1000 FXS driver and RJ11 service.

QMI/MBIM options · FXS line · optional SIP
05
ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Security & privacy

Firewall4/nftables, encrypted DNS and VPN inventory, plus a read-only posture review of standard WAN/WWAN zone defaults and redacted rule summaries. Enabled ACCEPT rules from WAN-like or unrestricted sources are flagged for review; full rule-order, NAT and packet-path analysis remain separate work.

WireGuard · OpenVPN · Unbound · DNS filtering · firewall posture review · rule inventory
06
RESEARCH

Hardware acceleration

Explore EDMA, PPE and Qualcomm NSS as a separate compatibility layer. Vanilla OpenWrt does not claim NSS acceleration.

Kernel compatibility · benchmarks · safe fallback
