QuantumGrid brings major router systems into one operating model. It is built around OpenWrt services and AW1000 hardware — not disconnected demo toggles.

01
FOUNDATION

Network & WAN

Interface status, IPv4/IPv6 default routes and policy-routing rules, multi-WAN visibility, DNS, DHCP and diagnostics. The Network page reads mwan3 interface/tracker state and per-target latency/loss separately from netifd link state, with search and CSV export, plus active IPv4/IPv6 policy weights and directly connected networks. Security Center also reports redacted WireGuard peer handshake ages and measured RX/TX rates without exposing peer keys or endpoint addresses. Peer rates use successive transfer-counter samples; first samples, resets, stale intervals and ambiguous duplicate peer identities remain unavailable. Each WAN reports measured RX/TX Mbps from successive kernel byte-counter samples, with unavailable rates shown when counters are missing, reset or stale; these are measured transfer rates, not link-speed estimates or policy traffic shares. The Advanced page also reports Netfilter connection-tracking table entries, configured maximum, free headroom and 75%/90% warning thresholds from read-only kernel counters; it keeps a bounded 30-sample session-only utilization trend and spreadsheet-safe CSV export. Unsupported counters remain unavailable rather than being shown as zero. Route and rule inventories are read-only kernel snapshots. A table-coverage view links lookup rules to reported default routes and flags missing table defaults for review without claiming the policy is invalid; these views do not change failover policy or simulate packet paths.

WAN/LAN visibility · mwan3 · ping · traceroute · mtr
02
FOUNDATION

Wi-Fi intelligence

A hardware-aware wireless layer for Qualcomm radios, with channel, client, band and roaming visibility. Existing AP interfaces now have authenticated SSID, WPA2/WPA3, hidden-network and driver-validated channel edits with Wi-Fi reload and rollback attempt. The AW1000 image includes upstream usteer and its native LuCI app for optional multi-AP roaming; the daemon is disabled by default until an administrator selects the ESS SSIDs and policy. Channel width/PHY mode and transmit-power controls are now driver-capability bounded; radio scheduling, automatic channel selection and a QuantumGrid-native roaming policy editor remain in development. Native LuCI usteer is installed for optional multi-AP steering and remains disabled until the administrator configures its SSIDs and policy.

Wi-Fi 6 · AP configuration · channel planning · usteer roaming
03
ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Gaming & latency

Configured SQM/CAKE queues and searchable/exportable kernel qdisc counters, plus bounded enable and rate controls for existing SQM queue sections. Updates validate rates, use the authenticated API and restart SQM, with a previous-value rollback attempt on restart failure. The five-packet fixed-target ICMP diagnostic reports packet loss and RTT variation. Controlled-load bufferbloat tests, CAKE tuning and per-client prioritisation remain in development.

SQM queue controls · CAKE telemetry · measured ping loss/jitter

HARDWARE INTEGRATION

5G & voice

Cellular modem management for RG500Q-EA, plus the AW1000 FXS driver and RJ11 service.

QMI/MBIM options · FXS line · optional SIP
05
ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Security & privacy

Firewall4/nftables, encrypted DNS and VPN inventory, plus a read-only posture review of standard WAN/WWAN zone defaults and redacted rule summaries. Enabled ACCEPT rules from WAN-like or unrestricted sources are flagged for review; WireGuard peer health now reports handshake age, transfer counters and allowed IPs without exporting keys or endpoint addresses. Full rule-order, NAT and packet-path analysis remain separate work.

WireGuard · OpenVPN · Unbound · DNS filtering · firewall posture review · rule inventory
06
EXPERIMENTAL PROFILE

Hardware acceleration

The stable firmware remains upstream OpenWrt without NSS. An opt-in AW1000 profile uses the pinned qosmio 25.12 NSS tree, NSS 12.5 firmware, the NSS core/ECM stack, AW1000 NSS device-tree nodes, EDMA/PPE switch support and ath11k/mac80211 Wi-Fi integration. The Advanced page reports component presence, but real NSS flow offload is not verified until packet counters and controlled hardware benchmarks pass. DSA bridge-VLAN filtering is incompatible with NSS Wi-Fi offload.

EDMA · PPE · NSS core · ECM · Wi-Fi offload · hardware validation required
